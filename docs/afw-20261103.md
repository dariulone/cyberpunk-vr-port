# AFW -- PureDark's alternate frame warping in the port (2026-11-03)

## What it is

PureDark's AFW (UEVR AFW, `PDAFWPlugin.dll`, closed, C API) renders ONE eye per frame, the eyes taking turns, and
makes the other eye for the same moment by warping: the drawn eye's colour reprojected across by its depth, and the
other eye's previous frame moved to now by the motion vectors where the drawn eye cannot see (`CombinedWarping`).
The port loads the plugin itself; the ABI is copied member for member in `include/Render/AfwPlugin.hpp`.

## The switch

`xr_afw=1` in `vrport.ini`, or *Stereo -> Stereo view -> AFW (PureDark frame warp)*; read once at start
(`Stereo/EngineStereo.cpp` ReadSwitch), so a change needs a restart. Engine stereo stays the default.

An AFW launch is a launch of the mono mode's kind -- no engine stereo, no second view (VRCAM), MAIN through the union
of the lens windows -- with two differences: MAIN is put at an eye, not the head centre (`IpdInWorldPos` 1), and
framegen is off (`Framegen.cpp` Enabled) while its input capture (depth and motion) stays on.

## How a frame goes

1. **Camera** (`Hooks/PatchCamera.cpp`, MAIN's write): `CyberpunkVR_MainIsRightEye = cvr::afw::FrameEye()`, which
   turns over with every move of the renderer's frame clock (renderer `+0x4CC8`). The eye goes into the written head
   pose (`OpenXRHeadPose::afwEye`) and travels with the picture in its pose label. Under a menu the eye is held.
2. **Capture** (`Runtimes/OpenXRCapture.cpp`, Present thread): the eye MAIN's picture is comes from its label, not
   the switch (the game thread is placing the next frame already). The AFW branch takes the frame's framegen inputs
   (`AcquireInputs(1, renderFrameId, ...)`, same queue), and `cvr::afw::Warp` records into the capture list: the
   picture, depth and motion into the plugin's buffers, `EvaluateFrameWarp`, the made eye into the second eye's slot.
   Both eyes are labelled from MAIN's label, each with its own eye pose; a frame whose label changed before the
   submission is not paired.
3. **Submit** (`Runtimes/OpenXRFrameLoop.cpp`): `CapturedMonoFrame::mainEye` routes the frame's two pictures, as
   the MAIN + second-view pair is routed.

Cameras for the plugin (`Render/Afw.cpp`): world-to-view from the framegen camera (right, up, -forward), the other
eye `2 * half IPD` along the camera's right; projections from the submitted OpenXR fovs with the engine's reversed
depth (near 1, far 0) and the depth texture as it is -- the conventions the offline replay validated
(`tools/afw_tests/afw_replay.cpp`). Motion scale = the engine's (a fraction of the image) x the eye's size.
Motion type: `FromOtherEye`, or `Normal` after a frame drawn for the same eye (counted, never seen so far).

## Measured (ManualSave-491, Megabuilding H10 street, simulator 90 Hz, RTX 5070 Ti, framegen off)

| launch        | game FPS | GPU busy | GPU per frame |
|---------------|---------:|---------:|--------------:|
| engine stereo |     66.2 |      98% |       14.8 ms |
| mono          |     68.6 |      50% |        7.3 ms |
| AFW           |     68.7 |      60% |        8.7 ms |

- The street is CPU-bound at ~68.6 FPS; AFW reaches the ceiling with ~40% of the GPU free. A GPU-bound scene (ray or
  path tracing, more pixels) is where it gains frames.
- The warp is 1.59 ms of GPU per frame (`CaptureSecond` in the GPU stage profile).
- Every frame warped, 0 fallbacks; eyes alternating on 550 of 550 consecutive frames still and 434 of 434 in a
  +-35 deg head sweep (54 FPS there); submission 90/s.
- Modes in a head sweep: `CombinedWarping` + `FromOtherEye` clean on the arms; `AlternateEyeWarping` tears hands at
  disocclusions; `Normal` motion type or no motion vectors doubles the moving arm. Strafe leaves thin streaks at the
  bottom edge (disocclusion fill).
- The HUD does not shake with the eyes taking turns: the HUD panels are put into each eye after the warp. The FPS
  counter's offset between the eyes stayed at -1 px over 16 stills with a far background and at 0 px over 12 with the
  near floor behind it.

## Tools

- `CyberpunkVR_AfwStats[6]`: warped, fallbacks, plugin state, alternating / same-eye / after-gap frames.
- `CyberpunkVR_AfwTune[8]` (mode, motion type (3 = auto), threshold x100, debug, clear, motion off) and
  `CyberpunkVR_AfwMotionScale[2]`, read every frame, for live A/B.
- `[afw]` lines in the port log: plugin load, eye buffers, first warp and every 5000th, fallbacks (powers of two).

## Open

- Whatever the engine itself draws flat into the frame is warped with the scene behind it (world-anchored marks
  are right that way).
- Menus and loading screens are one picture for both eyes.
